Research Projects

Penguin Behavioral Analytics (YOLOv8 Model)

Developed and optimized YOLOv8 models to detect and track penguins in livestream footage from the St. Louis Zoo. This research supports the study of behavioral analytics, stress detection, and automated health monitoring in exotic species. I collaborated with a graduate research team to enhance model accuracy and improve performance on large datasets.

Weed Detection in Remote Sensing Robotics (YOLOv11 Model)

Trained and validated Computer Vision YOLOv11 models with datasets of 900+ annotated images to distinguish between turnips and weeds for implementation onto remote sensing robotics. I contributed to the integration of these models into embedded systems, including software testing and deployment in field environments. This research focuses on improving automated weed detection systems to optimize agricultural practices.
