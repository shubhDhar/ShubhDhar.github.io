About Me

I’m a sophomore at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, majoring in Computer Science + Animal Sciences, and I’m excited to apply my skills to projects and research that bridge the gap between technology and life sciences.

I’m passionate about leveraging artificial intelligence and software development to address real-world challenges in health and biological systems. Currently, I’m conducting research under Dr. Isabella Condotta in the Animal Sciences Department, where I’ve developed and optimized YOLOv8 models to detect and track penguins in livestream footage from the St. Louis Zoo. Our work supports behavioral analytics, stress detection, and health monitoring in exotic species, and has sharpened my skills in Python, PyTorch, CUDA, Google Colab, and LabelStudio.

I also worked as a Remote Sensing Research Assistant under Dr. Sunoj Shajahan in the Agricultural and Biological Engineering Department. I trained YOLOv11 models on over 900 annotated images to distinguish between turnips and weeds for integration into autonomous robotics. These experiences have deepened my technical foundation and exposed me to the challenges of deploying machine learning in real-world agricultural contexts.

In addition to research, I served as a Course Assistant for CS124: Introduction to Computer Science I, helping over 150 students with programming in Java. This experience enhanced my communication, mentorship, and teaching skills.

Beyond academics, I’m the Executive Chair and Publicity Manager for The Other Guys, a premier a cappella group at UIUC. I manage our performances, website, and social media platforms, helping to grow our campus and online engagement.

I’m eager to grow in roles that combine my technical background with my passion for AI-driven innovation in the life sciences.
